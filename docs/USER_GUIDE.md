# Using the planner

## Describe the trip

Start on the Trip planner page. For example:

> Europe for 10 days in December 2026, leaving from LA and returning to LA. One adult, no checked bags, one carry-on.

You can name cities in order or keep a region flexible. The assistant asks one question at a time about missing details. Child and infant ages, including ages in months, affect the passenger categories. A city-count answer such as “I don't know, around four” applies to the active city question.

“I don't know,” “not sure” or “you decide” about city choices or moving pace delegates route selection to the planner. Regional routes remain open for comparison; explicitly chosen city orders are preserved. Missing departure cities, dates, passenger ages and baggage details are not invented.

## Automatic search

When a new chat response completes the required information, the planner shows a loading indicator and opens `/dashboard#transport`, where flight search begins automatically. There is no need to press Search flights to complete that conversation. The queue is consumed once and expires; reopening a dashboard does not by itself repeat a search.

The Search flights button remains available for existing drafts and manual retries. Incomplete trips stay in the conversation with the next needed question. Use New search for a separate trip idea.

## Compare routes and flights

The dashboard header links to the chosen trip, transport, accommodation, budget, map, schedule and route comparison. City comparisons show compact lowest-fare and shortest-flight summaries; after choosing a city itinerary, inspect the fuller set of returned flight combinations.

Expand a flight card for dated legs, airports, operating/marketing airline details where returned, layovers and their durations. Direct flights only applies to each leg. Every intermediate city transfer is searched, along with outbound and return legs.

Regional searches work in batches. Compare more routes or resume unfinished coverage from the dashboard. A partial search is not an exhaustive result. “Shortest flight time” can exclude layovers and destination time; it is not automatically the shortest door-to-door journey.

## Build the chosen trip

Add the flight or available train/bus options you want to the chosen trip. The route map connects the itinerary's ordered journeys rather than drawing every leg from the original departure airport. Choose transport to populate the schedule and city stay dates.

Search Hotels only, Hostels only, or both. Add a stay to the plan to include its quote in the estimated total. Selecting another property for the same city visit replaces the previous choice. The displayed airport distance is straight-line distance when coordinates are available, not a road journey or transfer-time estimate.

Totals are grouped by currency and include selected items. Unselected stays, missing baggage fees, transfers, activities and undisclosed charges are not silently included. Date/route changes can invalidate earlier choices and quotes.

## Saved trips

Open Saved trips in the main navigation to find, open, filter or delete trip ideas in one place. Single and bulk deletion, Undo and recently deleted recovery are available. Ideas and selections live in this browser's local storage, not a cloud account. Use the same browser/profile and origin to access them; clearing site data can remove them.

## Search results and purchases

Search results are quotes with retrieval times and coverage limitations. Separate one-way tickets do not imply protected connections or included baggage. Test-mode results must remain labeled. The application does not book flights, reserve accommodation, make payments, or guarantee the globally cheapest available trip.
